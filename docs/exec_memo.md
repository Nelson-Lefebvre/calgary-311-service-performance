# Memo: field crews are fast on new work and losing ground on old work

**To:** Operations managers, field business units (fictional audience)
**Data:** City of Calgary 311 requests, January 2022 to snapshot 2026-11-19 (development snapshot; final numbers on 2026-12-01)

## Answer

New 311 requests close as fast as they did in 2022-2024 for every field service except Fire. The problem is the old work: requests open for more than 90 days are at their highest October level since 2022, and most of them belong to Roads & Mobility.

## Three numbers

1. **34,661** field requests open 90+ days at the end of October 2026: +17% on October 2022, +32% on October 2025.
2. **77%** of those belong to Roads & Mobility, mostly scheduled repairs (curb and gutter, signs, back lanes).
3. **Fire** is the only field service that slipped: 82% of requests closed within benchmark in 2022-2024, 75% from 2025 to September 2026.

The benchmark is each service's own 80th percentile time to close in 2022-2024, because the City publishes no per-service targets.

## Impact

Residents with a scheduled repair wait months while dashboards built on new requests look healthy. Snow requests also arrive 1 to 3 days after a storm, not on the day, so winter staffing planned on same-day snowfall starts too early and ends too soon.

## Decisions requested

1. Roads & Mobility: review the oldest open repair requests and close stale records, so the backlog shows real pending work.
2. Fire: find what changed in 2025 in the services behind the drop before setting a target.
3. Winter operations: shift snow-response staffing one to three days after snowfall.

## Limits

"Closed" means closed in the system, not fixed on the ground. Some old open records may be stale, not pending work, so decision 1 comes first. Full caveats: [`data_quality.md`](data_quality.md), every number traced in [`evidence_log.md`](evidence_log.md).
